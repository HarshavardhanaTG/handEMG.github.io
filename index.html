Topology of surface electromyogram signals: hand gesture decoding on Riemannian manifolds

Harshavardhana T. Gowda1
Lee M. Miller1
1University of California, Davis
Paper A Paper B Code Data

Abstract

In this work, we collected one of the most demographically diverse EMG datasets to date, spanning 91 individuals across a wide range of ages, body mass indices, and physiological characteristics. Participants performed a variety of hand gestures, enabling us to systematically study the fundamental structure of surface EMG signals and quantify distribution shift across individuals.

Our results show that EMG signals are strongly shaped by physiological factors such as age, BMI, skin elasticity, and skin hydration. We further demonstrate that the manifold of symmetric positive definite matrices provides a natural embedding space for EMG signals, where distinct hand gestures form separable clusters. Together, these findings provide a geometric and physiologically grounded perspective on EMG variability, with implications for robust hand gesture decoding across diverse populations.

We continue to explore the geometry of EMG signals in our subsequent work, where we provide an algrebraic characterization of the geometry of EMG signals on Riemannian manifolds, see our Geometry of orofacial EMG.
